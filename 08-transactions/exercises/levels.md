# Exercise Levels: Transactions

## Level 1: Follow
Run a successful transfer and inspect balances before and after commit.

## Level 2: Modify
Force an error between two updates and confirm rollback.

## Level 3: Build
Implement atomic transfers in `code/exercise.py` with invariant tests.

## Level 4: Mastery
Analyze concurrent transfers, choose isolation behavior, and document the trade-off between correctness and throughput.
