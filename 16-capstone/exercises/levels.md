# Exercise Levels: Capstone

## Level 1: Follow
Build one vertical slice with a contract, code, and test.

## Level 2: Modify
Add persistence and preserve the first slice's invariant.

## Level 3: Build
Add authentication, background work, failure handling, and observability.

## Level 4: Mastery
Complete the production design, threat model, runbook, capacity plan, rollback plan, and retrospective.

## Completion Evidence
Record decisions in [experiment.md](experiment.md), review them in [answers.md](answers.md), and update [PROGRESS.md](../../PROGRESS.md).
