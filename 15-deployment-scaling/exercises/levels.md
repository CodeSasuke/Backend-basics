# Exercise Levels: Deployment And Scaling

## Level 1: Follow
Calculate a simple capacity estimate from concurrency and latency.

## Level 2: Modify
Change an assumption and explain how the estimate changes.

## Level 3: Build
Write a deployment design with health checks and limits.

## Level 4: Mastery
Defend a scaling architecture with capacity estimates, bottlenecks, failure modes, rollout, rollback, and cost.
