# Exercise Levels: Failure And Retries

## Level 1: Follow
Run the retry starter with a dependency that succeeds and one that fails.

## Level 2: Modify
Add a maximum total delay and classify non-retryable errors.

## Level 3: Build
Implement bounded backoff and idempotency behavior in `code/exercise.py`.

## Level 4: Mastery
Design deadlines, retry budgets, jitter, circuit breaking, fallback, and unknown-outcome handling for a side effect.
